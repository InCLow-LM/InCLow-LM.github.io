abstract: We investigate whether pre-training exclusively on dialogue data results
  in formally and functionally apt small language models. Based on this pre-trained
  llamalogue model, we employ a variety of fine-tuning strategies to enforce ``more
  communicative'' text generations by our models. Although our models underperform
  on most standard BabyLM benchmarks, they excel at dialogue continuation prediction
  in a minimal pair setting. While PPO fine-tuning has mixed to adversarial effects
  on our models, DPO fine-tuning further improves their performance on our custom
  dialogue benchmark.
